OUR IDEA

Not Just Another Chocolate.

We started with a simple question: What if a familiar Indian flavour could be experienced in an entirely new way?

GULMELT is an early product concept exploring the combination of rich chocolate, aromatic gulkand and the texture of dry fruits.

We are currently developing and testing the concept before launch.
